Sources & caveats

Data: MODIS Terra Level-3 monthly NDVI (1 km), served by NASA GIBS, sampled here at ~4.7 km. Each pixel's palette index is decoded to its NDVI bin midpoint.

White = NDVI near zero (open water, bare ground). Black inside the border = no valid data that month.

Gap: NASA has not published April 2025; that frame is an average of March and May (hollow dot on the chart).

Brazil mean: area-weighted (cos² lat) over pixels inside the border. Border: Natural Earth via world-atlas.
