The 2026 World Tour

23 rounds, Melbourne (Mar 8) to Abu Dhabi (Dec 6). Each circuit is colored by the team that won there.

Sources & methodology

Results are official through Round 14 (Spanish GP, Madrid, Sep 13); circuits without a result yet are hollow grey and can still change dates. Constructor points and wins are the standings after that round. Circuit locations are the venues' real coordinates (track/paddock center); the Madrid street circuit (IFEMA "Madring") is newly built for 2026 and shown at its announced site.

The calendar has changed since it was announced: the Bahrain and Saudi Arabian Grands Prix were cancelled after the outbreak of the Iran war, and the Bahrain Grand Prix was reinstated at Sepang, Malaysia (Oct 4) under its original name. The map shows the 23 rounds now scheduled; the two Middle East venues are not drawn. The Spanish GP moved to Madrid (Round 14), while Barcelona-Catalunya is its own round (Round 7).

Track outlines in the calendar are circuit centerlines from the bacinger/f1-circuits GeoJSON dataset, simplified and scaled to fit each card (not drawn to a common scale); the Madrid outline is the announced Madring layout.

The dashed route is a great-circle path between consecutive rounds — the shortest path over the globe, not the actual freight routing; brighter legs are races already run. Coastlines are Natural Earth 1:110m via the world-atlas TopoJSON build, rendered client-side with D3 geoNaturalEarth1 — no map API or access token involved.

Sources: Formula1.com race results · f1-fansite standings · Formula1.com, Malaysia joins calendar · world-atlas (Natural Earth) · bacinger/f1-circuits (track outlines)
