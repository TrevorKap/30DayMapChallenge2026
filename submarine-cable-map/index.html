Sources & caveats

Cable routes, lengths, ready-for-service years and owners: TeleGeography Submarine Cable Map (CC BY-SA 4.0). Land: world-atlas (Natural Earth). Routes are schematic, not survey-grade.

Line weight is a proxy: cables from later years carry far more data by design, but no source publishes per-cable capacity or live traffic, so weight follows ready-for-service year, not measured volume.

The dataset lists cables in service or planned. Retired cables are missing, so earlier years show only the survivors, not everything that existed then.

"Big Tech owner" means Google, Meta/Facebook, Microsoft or Amazon appears in a cable's owner list; most are consortium cables, so ownership is shared. The "almost all" figure is an industry estimate, commonly put at 95–99%.
