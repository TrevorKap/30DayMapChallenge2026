A Very Balkan Roadtrip

8Cities· 7Countries· 2Weeks· 1Trip

Eight cities, seven countries, two weeks, one loop — out of Sarajevo and back again. The route runs Belgrade, Sofia, Skopje, Pristina, Durrës, Budva, and Mostar, threading Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Albania, and Montenegro. Kosovo's border is drawn dashed, reflecting its contested international status; neighboring countries are shown for context only.

Itinerary

Each country carries one iconic monument as a small line-art badge — Stari Most, Kalemegdan Fortress, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Skopje's Porta Macedonia, the Newborn monument, Krujë Castle, and Ostrog Monastery. Ostrog sits at its real coordinates; the rest are placed in open space within their country rather than pinned to the exact building, so they don't collide with the route or the numbered stops.

The route is drawn along real highways: each leg between stops is a live driving route from OSRM, routed over OpenStreetMap's own road network, on top of a live Mapbox basemap. If a stretch is ever unreachable by the routing engine (an offline demo server, a network hiccup), that stretch falls back to a straight illustrative line rather than breaking the loop. Day counts are a suggested pace across a two-week loop, not a fixed itinerary. Country fills mark the seven nations the trip crosses — Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Albania, and Montenegro; surrounding countries are shown for geographic context only. Kosovo's boundary is rendered as a dashed line, a common cartographic convention for territories with disputed or partially recognized international status, per the same Mapbox country boundaries dataset used to draw every other border on this map.
