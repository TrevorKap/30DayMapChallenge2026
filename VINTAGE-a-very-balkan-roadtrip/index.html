A Very Balkan Roadtrip

8Cities· 7Countries· 2Weeks· 1Trip

Eight cities, seven countries, two weeks, one loop — out of Sarajevo and back again. The route runs Belgrade, Sofia, Skopje, Pristina, Durrës, Budva, and Mostar, threading Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Albania, and Montenegro. Kosovo's border is drawn dashed, reflecting its contested international status; neighboring countries are shown for context only.

Each stop marker is a real cropped photo of that city's own landmark, sitting at the city's real coordinates: an aerial view of Sarajevo from Žuta Tabija, Belgrade's Genex Tower, Sofia's Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, the Alexander the Great statue in Skopje, Pristina's National Library, the old town walls of Durrës, Budva's walled old town, and Mostar's Stari Most. Photos are sourced from Wikimedia Commons under their Creative Commons or public-domain licenses.

The route is drawn along real highways: each leg between stops is a live driving route from OSRM, routed over OpenStreetMap's own road network, on top of a live Mapbox basemap. If a stretch is ever unreachable by the routing engine (an offline demo server, a network hiccup), that stretch falls back to a straight illustrative line rather than breaking the loop. Country fills mark the seven nations the trip crosses — Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Albania, and Montenegro; surrounding countries are shown for geographic context only. Kosovo's boundary is rendered as a dashed line, a common cartographic convention for territories with disputed or partially recognized international status, per the same Mapbox country boundaries dataset used to draw every other border on this map.
